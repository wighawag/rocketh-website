# Frequently asked questions

This page has no `order`, so the sidebar places it after the pages that have one.

## Can a page use curly braces?

Yes: {this} is just text, and so is `{#not-an-anchor}` in the middle of a sentence. Prose is never compiled as Svelte.

## Does a colon in prose survive?

It does: a ratio of 16:9, a time of 10:30, and a label:value pair all read as written.
